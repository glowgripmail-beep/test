# Copywriting and Ads (Hebrew DR, visions, Meta Ads)

This skill merges three sources:

- **hebrew-dr-source**: only its niche-neutral parts. That means the Hebrew DR copy process, voice, AI-tells, formats, final check, the DR methodology and its authorities, the vision-to-copy rules, and the method for offers, pricing and unit economics. Everything specific to the original business was removed: its product, prices, avatars, competitors, swipes and supplement or health knowledge. Niche examples were turned into `[bracketed placeholders]` that keep their structure. The content now lives in topic files under `references/`.
- **ad-vision-writing**: how to write the vision brief itself. This means the twelve components, devices, the Hebrew skeleton and a worked example. Folder: `ad-vision-writing/`.
- **meta-ads-analysis**: the profit-first operating system for reading Meta Ads data. Folder: `meta-ads-analysis/`.

Each file marks its origin in `<!-- from: ... -->` comments.

Before writing any copy, read the working-style and request-type rules: `references/hebrew-dr-copy.md` parts 0, 1 and 2. When the user's business has its own dated product and offer sheet or allowed-claims document, that document fills every `[placeholder]` and takes priority over the generalized examples here. See `references/hebrew-dr-copy.md`, section 1.

## Standing rules that apply across all parts

These are stated in the sources. They are summarized here only so they are not missed. The full wording is in the files named.

- Exactly three hooks per copy request. See `references/dr-methodology.md` and `references/ads-and-vision.md` rule 3; the exceptions are in `references/hebrew-dr-copy.md` §2.
- No dashes as punctuation in copy or in answers. Hebrew by default. See `references/hebrew-dr-copy.md` 0.1 and §6.
- Never invent quotes, customers, numbers, studies, reviews or scarcity. Mark what is missing as `[לאשר: ...]`. See `references/hebrew-dr-copy.md` §6 and §7.
- Meta Ads: follow the order of operations tracking -> profit -> funnel -> creative -> decision. Never judge on broken tracking or on fewer than about 10 purchases. See `meta-ads-analysis/GUIDE.md`.

## Conflicts between sources (not resolved; ask the user when one matters)

1. **Number of hooks.** dr-methodology.md: "Every copy request must include exactly three hooks... This applies to every deliverable that involves copy." hebrew-dr-copy.md §2 (thickup SKILL.md): a spot edit gets no hooks, a CTA-only request gets no hooks, and "הוק אחד רק אם ביקשו 'רק אחד'".
2. **"Exclusive" methodology vs. other frameworks.** dr-methodology.md says it is "the **exclusive** methodology for all copy work. Do not import outside frameworks". hebrew-dr-copy.md (thickup SKILL.md) uses LF8, Cialdini and Hormozi. ad-vision-writing (anatomy.md, devices.md) uses the Hawkins scale of consciousness.
3. **Default ad structure.** dr-methodology.md: "AIDA: ... Default for ads and product pages" (with PAS and BAB as alternatives). ads-and-vision.md §4.2: an ad script "חייב לעבוד לפי מבנה רמות המודעות של המנטור" (desire hook, calm dissonance, courage, soft quiz CTA). hebrew-dr-copy.md §3ב: that four-role skeleton is the default, and "ויז'ן יכול לסדר אחרת".
4. **Reference structure vs. house skeleton.** ads-and-vision.md rule 2: "הוויז'ן הוא התבנית... לא לסטות מהמבנה בלי סיבה". ad-vision-writing/GUIDE.md "House structure": "When the reference fights the skeleton, the skeleton wins".
5. **Quiz CTA.** ad-vision-writing/GUIDE.md component 10: "CTA. In our house style, soft and quiz-like." The same applies to its house structure and to ads-and-vision.md §3.6 ("CTA רך של שאלון"). hebrew-dr-copy.md: "שאלון ב CTA רק אם אושר שקיים" and "CTA רך עם ההצעה בפנים... CTA בלי הצעה חלש".
6. **Money back in the courage beat.** ad-vision-writing/GUIDE.md House structure ("money back") and the template.md worked example ("הכסף חוזר") include it. hebrew-dr-copy.md §6 and part ז say not to invent "החזר מלא", and to use it only if approved, otherwise `[לאשר]`.
7. **Naming the enemy.** ad-vision-writing/GUIDE.md: "Name the enemy out loud". hebrew-dr-copy.md §6: do not attack legitimate professionals or alternatives. offers-pricing-unit-economics.md: the expensive alternative may appear only as a spending comparison, never as a claim of similar results.
8. **One version vs. several.** ad-vision-writing/GUIDE.md "Batches": several visions must cover different awareness rungs. hebrew-dr-copy.md 0.2 #9 and ads-and-vision.md rule 5 prefer one precise version over several half-baked ones. hebrew-dr-copy.md §3ב: without a concept, weigh 2 to 3 directions and pick one.
9. **Explaining the mechanism.** ads-and-vision.md rule 1 bans sentences that explain the mechanism like a lecture; the mechanism must come out through personal experience. ad-vision-writing/references/template.md example: "הדיסוננס... נאמר בטון של הסבר ולא של גילוי". ads-and-vision.md §3.6 calls for "דיסוננס מדעי רגוע (semi-scientific)".
10. **The "wrong thing treated" dissonance.** In the original template.md example, the dissonance was the "worked from the outside / the body prioritizes [the thing] last" argument. The original thickup SKILL.md banned that exact argument for its product as unsupported. In the generalized example the argument is a placeholder, so the banned-argument rule in hebrew-dr-copy.md §6 decides what can fill it.
11. **Percentages in hooks.** ads-and-vision.md rule 3 lists percentages ("מעל 98%") as a hook type. hebrew-dr-copy.md §6 forbids inventing percentages and requires the accurate version of any distorted statistic.
12. **VAT in profit math.** meta-ads-analysis/GUIDE.md Step 2 says to strip 18% VAT before ROAS. offers-pricing-unit-economics.md says an עוסק פטור does **not** strip VAT. The GUIDE now states this as a condition.
13. **Vision length.** ad-vision-writing says 150 to 350 words, and template.md adds "אם עברת 400, יש שם שני וויז'נים". ads-and-vision.md §4.1 adds part A (the reference analysis) to the same document. ad-vision-writing/GUIDE.md "חיבורים" says the limits apply to part B only.
14. **Twelve vs. thirteen components.** ad-vision-writing/GUIDE.md lists twelve components. template.md's skeleton has thirteen items: it adds the concept name as item 1 and splits nothing else.
15. **Dashes.** hebrew-dr-copy.md bans dashes as punctuation in copy and in answers. The English prose of ad-vision-writing/GUIDE.md and meta-ads-analysis/GUIDE.md uses em dashes. This is source text, not copy.
