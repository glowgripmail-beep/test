# Consumer Psychology

People buy on emotion and justify with logic. This skill explains the emotion. Use it to choose angles, diagnose weak creatives, and ground copy feedback — never as a replacement for the DR methodology in copywriting-ads (references/dr-methodology.md).

## LF8 — the 8 biological desires (Whitman, Cashvertising)

Every angle must connect to at least one. The stronger and more visual the connection, the stronger the ad.

1. Survival, enjoyment of life, life extension
2. Enjoyment of food and beverages
3. **Freedom from fear, pain, and danger** (incl. social fear — embarrassment)
4. **Sexual companionship / attractiveness**
5. Comfortable living conditions
6. **To be superior — winning, keeping up**
7. Care and protection of loved ones
8. **Social approval**

Appearance and beauty products live mostly on 4, 8, 6, 3 ([a visible appearance problem] especially: 3 social fear/embarrassment, 4 attractiveness, 8 social approval). Health and energy products add 1. Map every avatar-angle pair to its LF8 before writing.

## The 9 learned (secondary) wants

To be informed · curiosity · cleanliness of body · efficiency · convenience · dependability/quality · expression of beauty and style · economy/profit · bargains. Use these to sharpen offers and product-page copy; they support, LF8 drives.

Whitman's three near-universal appeals: **fast, easy, cheap**. Strong copy answers "how fast will I understand?", "how easy is it to start?", "how much does it cost to try?"

## Persuasion principles (Cialdini) — as diagnostic checklist

- **Social proof** — people copy people like them (matching-avatar UGC > celebrity)
- **Authority** — expertise signals, mechanism explanation
- **Scarcity / loss aversion** — losses hurt ~2× more than gains please; frame what they LOSE by waiting
- **Reciprocity** — free value first (gifts, guide, content)
- **Commitment & consistency** — small yes before big yes (quiz, micro-engagement)
- **Liking** — similarity + relatability of the presenter
- **Unity** — shared identity ("ישראלים כמוך")

## Biases that move e-com conversion

- **Anchoring** — the first price seen frames everything; always show the anchor before the real price
- **Decoy/comparison** — cost-per-day vs. the expensive alternative ([expensive professional service], [procedure], [recurring paid alternative]) reframes "expensive" to "cheap"
- **Endowment via imagination** — make them mentally own it ("תדמייני את המראה אחרי...")
- **Ambiguity aversion** — uncertainty kills checkout; guarantee + clear shipping remove it
- **Cognitive ease / processing fluency** — confusion = no sale; one idea per ad, one CTA. A concrete specific sentence reads as more true than a vague one

## Objection psychology

Every objection is an emotional risk wearing a logical mask:
- "יקר לי" = fear of wasting money / being a sucker → value stack, guarantee, cost-per-use
- "זה לא יעבוד" = fear of disappointment (again) → mechanism + proof + others-like-me
- "זה לא בשבילי" = identity mismatch → matching-avatar testimonial
- "אחר כך" = no felt cost of waiting → consequence of inaction + real deadline

Diagnose which risk the copy failed to defuse — that's the fix.

## Pricing psychology

Anchor high → reveal price · charm pricing (X99/X90) · bundle to blur unit price · installments framing ("3× ₪66" feels smaller than ₪199) · cost-per-use reframe · guarantee shifts risk from buyer to seller.

## How to use in creative testing

For each creative, log: avatar × LF8 desire × awareness stage × angle × emotion targeted. Failed creative? Check in order: (1) wrong/weak LF8, (2) awareness mismatch, (3) unbelievable claim (proof gap), (4) no felt cost of inaction.
