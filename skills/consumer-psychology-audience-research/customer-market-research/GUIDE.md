# Customer & Market Research

Goal: harvest the customer's **own language** and map the market, so copy channels existing desire onto the product (Schwartz: copy doesn't create desire — it channels it). Research output feeds directly into avatars and copy written per the copywriting-ads skill (methodology in references/dr-methodology.md).

## Core principle

Never invent customer language. Every pain, desire, and objection in a research doc should trace to real verbatim quotes or observed behavior. Specific quotes > general assumptions.

## Sources to mine (in priority order)

1. **Competitor reviews** — Amazon, AliExpress, [category-specific marketplaces], local Israeli stores. Gold mine: 1–3 star reviews reveal objections and broken promises; 5-star reveal the felt benefit in customer words.
2. **Meta Ad Library** — competitors' longest-running ads = proven angles. Log hooks, claims, offers.
3. **Comments on competitor ads/videos** — objections, skepticism, questions asked repeatedly.
4. **Facebook groups / Reddit / forums** — how people describe the problem when no one is selling to them.
5. **TikTok/YouTube comments** on related content.
6. **Own data** — post-purchase surveys, WhatsApp/support chats, abandoned-cart reasons, existing reviews.
7. **Surveys** — only after mining; use to validate, not to discover.

## What to extract (VOC categories)

Tag every quote into one of:
- **Pains** — the problem in their words (not clinical terms)
- **Dream outcome** — the after-state, emotional end result
- **Objections** — price / skepticism / "not for me" / timing / trust
- **Trigger events** — what made them start looking NOW
- **Failed solutions** — what they tried and why it disappointed
- **Identity statements** — "אני מסוג האנשים ש..."
- **Steal-worthy phrases** — vivid lines to reuse verbatim in copy

## Process

1. Define 3–5 research questions (what decisions will this research drive?).
2. Collect 50–100+ verbatim quotes across sources. Keep original language.
3. Tag by VOC category and by avatar.
4. Rank by **frequency × intensity** — what comes up most and hurts most.
5. Map the market: awareness stage of each avatar + market sophistication stage (how saturated are the claims).
6. Build a **competitor claim matrix**: competitor × (hook types, claims, mechanism, offer, proof). Identify the gap no one owns.
7. Write the research document (template below) ending with copy implications.

## Research document template

```
# [Brand/Product] — Customer Research
## Research questions
## Avatar summaries (per avatar: demo, pain, dream outcome, objections, awareness stage)
## VOC bank (quotes by category, with source)
## Frequency × intensity ranking
## Market sophistication assessment
## Competitor claim matrix + gap
## Copy implications (angles to test, phrases to use, objections to answer, claims to avoid)
```

## The five depth questions per avatar

Instead of building around age/gender/city, build around: (1) what do they want now; (2) what hurts now; (3) what have they already tried; (4) why has it not been solved; (5) what must they believe in order to move forward.

## Survey question design

Open questions only, past-behavior over intention ("מה ניסית עד היום?" not "האם היית קונה?"), one topic per question, never lead the witness. 5–7 questions max.
