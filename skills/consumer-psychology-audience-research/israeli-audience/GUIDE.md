# The Israeli Audience

> Note: some examples address a female shopper ("אל תשלמי", "ישראליות"); adapt the gender to the target audience.

How Israeli consumers think, what builds their trust, and what kills it. Use for strategy, creative direction, and interpreting research — copy itself follows the copywriting-ads skill.

## The Israeli consumer psyche

- **Freier anxiety (פחד פראייר)** — the deepest Israeli consumer fear is being a sucker. Stronger than price sensitivity itself. Answer with: transparency, proof, guarantee, "אל תשלמי יותר ב[האלטרנטיבה היקרה]", counterfeit warnings.
- **Default skepticism** — assume the reader thinks it's a scam until proven otherwise. Hype language accelerates distrust; dugri (ישיר ותכלס) builds it.
- **Dugri culture** — Israelis reward directness. Say the price, say the catch, say what it does. Translated-English "advertising Hebrew" reads as fake instantly.
- **"אנשים כמוני" proof** — Israelis trust local, imperfect, real people over polish. Israeli names, Israeli accents, Israeli homes in UGC. "[מספר אמיתי] ישראליות" beats "[מספר אמיתי] לקוחות".
- **Bargain identity** — finding a deal is a sport and a bragging right. Frame offers as a win the buyer achieved, not charity from the brand.

## Language rules (Hebrew)

- Conversational modern Hebrew; slang calibrated to avatar age (18–25 vs 35+ is a different dialect).
- **Gendered singular address** — את/אתה must match the avatar; mixed/plural address feels like a billboard, not a friend.
- Read aloud test: if it sounds like a radio ad, rewrite.

## Trust signals that convert in Israel

Money-back guarantee · Israeli WhatsApp support number · חנות רשמית / official-store framing + counterfeit warning · shipping stated in days ("עד 3 ימי עסקים") — Israelis were burned by AliExpress waits · local reviews with real Israeli names · clear return policy.

## Checkout & payments

- **תשלומים (installments)** expected above ~₪150; display them ("3 תשלומים ללא ריבית").
- Bit is mainstream; offering it lifts trust.
- Every extra field in checkout = drop-off; Israelis abandon fast.

## Regulation (must respect)

- Consumer Protection Law: 14-day cancellation right on distance sales — hiding it damages trust and is illegal.

## The Israeli calendar (plan campaigns around it)

- **Rosh Hashana & Pesach** — biggest gifting/self-renewal shopping peaks ("חדש לחג").
- **November** — Shopping IL, 11.11, Black Friday: Israelis now wait for it; run real offers.
- **Hanukkah** — gifts; **ט"ו באב** — local Valentine's; **יום המשפחה** — family gifting.
- August (back-to-school squeeze) and chagim aftermath = wallet fatigue; adjust expectations.
- **Security situation sensitivity** — during escalations, pause upbeat creatives, tone-check everything, never exploit the news.

## Segments to remember

Secular / traditional / religious / Haredi differ in imagery and modesty norms — check creative fit per targeting. Center vs periphery affects shipping expectations and price sensitivity. Russian- and Arabic-speaking segments exist and are underserved.

## Platforms

Facebook still strong 30+ · Instagram 18–35 core · TikTok dominant under 25 and growing · WhatsApp is the expected customer-service channel, not email.
